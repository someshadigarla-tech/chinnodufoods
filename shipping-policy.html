5-Layer Protective Food Packaging Standards

Traditional Andhra pickles are packed with aromatic wood-pressed oils and authentic spices. To guarantee zero oil leakage, aroma retention, and breakage-free delivery:

  1. Food-Grade Virgin PET / Glass Jars: High-density, chemical-free food containers certified under FSSAI norms.
  2. Induction Foil Hermetic Heat Seal: Machine-pressed foil barrier that locks in authentic village aromas and prevents any fluid seepage.
  3. Secondary Aroma Barrier Wrap: Thermal shrink-wrap encasing the jar lid.
  4. Heavy-Duty Bubble Cushioning: Dual-layer air-bubble protective cushioning absorbing transit impacts.
  5. Double-Wall Corrugated Carton: Sealed with tamper-evident Chinnodu Foods branded security tape.
5

Live Tracking & Delivery Notifications

As soon as your parcel is handed over to our courier partner:

  • You will receive an automated notification containing your Airway Bill (AWB) / Consignment Tracking Number.
  • You can monitor your parcel live on our dedicated tracking page: chinnodufoods.com/track.html simply by entering your Order ID (e.g. CF-84291) or your mobile number.
  • Direct 1-click links to the official DTDC, Delhivery, or Speed Post tracking portals are embedded right on the tracking page.
6

Customer Availability & Non-Delivery

Our courier partners attempt delivery up to two (2) times during business hours. The delivery executive will contact you on the mobile phone number provided during checkout.

If a parcel cannot be delivered due to recipient unavailability, refusal to accept, or an incomplete address, the parcel is held at the local courier hub for 48 hours before return. Please coordinate with the delivery executive or message our customer care team promptly.
