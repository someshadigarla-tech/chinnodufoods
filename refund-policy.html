Cancellation & Refund Policy

We stand behind the authentic taste, hygiene, and freshness of every jar with our 100% Replacement and Zero-Hassle Refund Guarantee.

1

Perishable Food Products Disclaimer

In accordance with the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) regulations and standard e-commerce food safety protocols, perishable culinary products once opened, unsealed, or consumed cannot be physically returned to our cloud kitchens.

However, our customers are our extended family! If your shipment arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect, you do NOT need to ship anything back to us. We will immediately issue a free replacement fresh batch or process a 100% full financial refund to your account.

2

Eligible Situations for 100% Replacement or Refund

You are fully entitled to a replacement batch or a 100% monetary refund in any of the following instances:

  • Transit Damage or Leakage: Jars cracked, broken, or induction seal torn resulting in oil leakage during courier handling.
  • Wrong Delicacy Dispatched: If you received an item different from your order receipt (e.g. received Gongura Pickle instead of Chicken Pickle, or different weight option).
  • Quality or Seal Compromise: Foil vacuum seal was unsealed or damaged prior to opening.
  • Confirmed Courier Loss: Package officially declared lost or untraceable in transit by the courier partner after 7 business days.
💎 Zero Deductions Guarantee

When a refund is approved due to damaged parcels or wrong dispatches, we refund 100% of your payment, including any delivery fees paid. No cancellation penalties or restocking charges are ever deducted.

3

How to File a Claim (Simple 3-Step Process)

To claim a replacement or refund, please follow these simple steps within 24 hours of parcel delivery:

  1. Capture Visual Proof: Take 2 to 3 clear photos or a short video showing the outer shipping label, the damaged area, or the incorrect jar.
  2. Message Our Priority Support: Send the photos along with your Order ID (e.g. CF-84291) to our dedicated WhatsApp Support line at +91 96766 98427 or email orders@chinnodufoods.com.
  3. Immediate Resolution: Our customer support lead reviews your claim within 2 to 4 hours. Upon verification, your free replacement batch will be freshly prepared and dispatched, or your refund initiated immediately!
4

Refund Processing Timelines & Mode of Settlement

All refunds are transferred directly back to the original funding source:

  • UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM): 24 to 48 hours from the time refund is initiated by our accounts team.
  • Net Banking / Debit Cards: 3 to 5 business banking days, subject to your issuing bank’s internal clearance cycle.
  • Transaction Reference Notification: You will receive an SMS and WhatsApp confirmation containing the UPI Bank Reference Number (RRN) once the refund is executed.
5

Order Cancellation Before Dispatch

If you placed an order by mistake or wish to cancel your order:

  • You may cancel free of charge within two (2) hours of order placement by messaging us on WhatsApp at +91 96766 98427 or calling our kitchen desk.
  • Once our homemakers have begun small-batch kitchen cooking or packaged your order with induction seal, cancellations cannot be accommodated due to the perishable nature of homemade foods.
6

Non-Refundable Circumstances

Refunds or replacements cannot be granted under the following scenarios:

  • Orders delivered successfully to the address provided, but reported after 24 hours of delivery.
  • Parcels returned due to multiple failed delivery attempts caused by an incorrect/incomplete address or continuous refusal to receive the parcel.
  • Personal subjective taste preferences (e.g. “less spicy than expected”), as authentic Andhra recipes follow traditional, balanced village spice profiles described on product pages.
