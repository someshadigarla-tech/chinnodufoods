Introduction & Our Privacy Pledge

Welcome to Chinnodu Foods (“we”, “our”, or “us”), accessible at chinnodufoods.com. We are an authentic Andhra traditional food enterprise and cloud kitchen dedicated to handcrafting pure village-style pickles, sweets, and savouries.

This Privacy Policy describes our practices regarding the collection, storage, protection, and disclosure of personal data when you visit our website, communicate with our customer support, or place orders for our homemade delicacies. We operate strictly in compliance with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 (“SPDI Rules”), and the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020.

2

Information We Collect

To ensure timely kitchen preparation, food-safe packaging, and pan-India courier delivery, we collect only the necessary information:

  • Contact & Identification Data: Full Name, Mobile Phone Number, and Email Address. Phone numbers are utilized for two-factor verification (OTP), order progress tracking, and courier delivery coordination.
  • Delivery & Shipping Address: House/flat number, street address, landmark, city, district, state, and 6-digit Indian PIN code to enable courier partners (DTDC, Delhivery, India Post) to deliver to your doorstep.
  • Transaction & Order Details: Selected delicacies, package weight options (250g, 500g, 1kg), order value, transaction reference numbers, and delivery instructions.
  • Device & Usage Information: Non-personally identifiable diagnostic data including browser type, operating system, and IP address collected automatically via server logs for system security and DDOS mitigation.
🔒 100% Zero-Storage of Financial Credentials

We NEVER collect or store debit/credit card numbers, CVVs, net-banking passwords, or UPI PINs. All payments are processed directly through certified, RBI-licensed payment gateways and UPI protocols (BHIM, PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm) featuring bank-grade 256-bit encryption.

3

How We Use Your Information

Your personal data is used solely for legitimate e-commerce operations, including:

  1. Order Processing & Kitchen Coordination: Freshly preparing your selected pickles and sweets, packing in food-grade airtight jars, and generating tax invoices.
  2. Courier Dispatch & Tracking: Generating airway bills (AWB), scheduling pickup with logistics partners, and transmitting real-time SMS / WhatsApp delivery tracking alerts.
  3. Customer Care & Grievance Resolution: Responding to inquiries, assisting with order status lookups, and addressing any transit-related issues.
  4. Security & Fraud Prevention: Preventing fraudulent order requests and protecting our cloud platform from malicious activity.
4

Zero Data Selling Guarantee

We do NOT sell, lease, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information to third-party marketing agencies, advertisers, or data aggregators under any circumstances.

Your data is only shared with authorized service partners strictly necessary to complete your order:

  • Logistics & Courier Partners: DTDC, Delhivery, India Post (Speed Post) for shipping your food parcel.
  • Payment Processors: RBI-authorized payment aggregators for processing UPI transactions.
  • Regulatory & Legal Authorities: Disclosed only when mandated by applicable Indian laws or court orders.
5

Data Storage & Cryptographic Security

We implement industry-leading technical and physical security safeguards to protect customer records against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction:

  • Cryptographic Salting: All admin session tokens and verification codes are generated via Cryptographically Secure Pseudorandom Number Generators (CSPRNG) and salted SHA-256 hashes.
  • SSL/TLS Encryption: All communication between your browser and our servers is encrypted using modern TLS 1.3 encryption.
  • Dual-Mode Data Redundancy: Order documents are maintained with point-in-time snapshot vaults and cloud replica sets to prevent data loss.
6

Cookies & Local Storage

Our website uses essential client-side LocalStorage and session cookies exclusively to:

  • Preserve your chosen delicacies and quantities in the shopping cart across page refreshes.
  • Facilitate instant order status lookups without requiring a manual account registration.

We do not utilize invasive cross-site tracking cookies. You may clear your browser cookies and local storage at any time via your browser settings.

7

Your Rights & Data Deletion Requests

As a valued customer, you hold the right to:

  • Request an extract of your personal contact records held in our order database.
  • Request correction or rectification of any incomplete or inaccurate delivery address.
  • Request permanent deletion of your customer profile and historical contact records (subject to retention required for accounting, GST taxation, and FSSAI audits).
