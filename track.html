Frequently Asked Questions About Delivery

⏱️ When is my courier tracking ID generated?

Because our sweets and pickles are prepared fresh daily, cooking and aroma-sealing takes 12 to 24 hours. Your DTDC or Delhivery tracking consignment number is activated as soon as the courier pickup scan is completed.

📦 How long will delivery take to my city?

Deliveries within Andhra Pradesh & Telangana arrive within 2 to 3 days. South India takes 3 to 4 days, while Mumbai, Delhi NCR, and other states take 4 to 6 days via express air courier.

🧾 Can I download my GST Tax Invoice?

Yes! Click the green “Print / Download Tax Invoice (PDF)” button that appears above once your order is displayed to immediately save or print your official invoice.
