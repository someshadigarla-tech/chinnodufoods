Our Heritage & Roots

From Our Village Kitchen to Your Dining Table

In our hometown, pickle-making and sweet preparation wasn't just cooking—it was a cherished community festival. Every summer, hand-picked raw sour mangoes were cut under shade, sun-dried Guntur chillies were ground on traditional granite stone mortars, and aromatic cold wood-pressed groundnut oil was poured generously into ceramic jaadis.

At Chinnodu Foods, we founded this initiative with one pure vision: to bring that authentic, chemical-free, mother's village taste to every city home, ensuring our heritage recipes continue to nourish upcoming generations.
